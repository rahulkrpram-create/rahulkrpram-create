## 🚀 Key Open-Source Projects & Scientific Repositories
### 1. [`astrophysics-reasoning-benchmarks`](https://github.com/rahulkrpram/astrophysics-reasoning-benchmarks)
* **Domain:** Relativistic Astrophysics & AI Alignment
* **Stack:** Python, Astropy, SymPy, LaTeX
* **Overview:** A specialized benchmark suite containing 150+ multi-step Chain-of-Thought problems evaluating frontier reasoning models (OpenAI o1/o3, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Gemini 2.0 Pro) on orbital eccentricity, relativistic perihelion advance, Tolman-Oppenheimer-Volkoff (TOV) equations, and black hole event horizon bounds.
### 2. [`lean4-physics-formalization`](https://github.com/rahulkrpram/lean4-physics-formalization)
* **Domain:** Formal Verification & Proof Assistants
* **Stack:** Lean 4, Mathlib, Z3 SMT Solver
* **Overview:** Machine-checked interactive proofs formalizing classical mechanics, Kepler's laws, and Hamiltonian conservation principles in dependent type theory. Enables programmatic verification of AI-generated derivations, catching skipped algebraic steps and hallucinations.
### 3. [`exoplanet-transit-photometry`](https://github.com/rahulkrpram/exoplanet-transit-photometry)
* **Domain:** Observational Astronomy & Time-Series Photometry
* **Stack:** Python, Astropy, Lightkurve, SciPy, Matplotlib
* **Overview:** Automated data processing pipeline for space telescope light curves. Fits transit parameters, computes quadratic limb-darkening coefficients, and extracts planet-to-star radius ratios while filtering out background eclipsing binaries.
